About

I am a Senior Data Analyst from Russia, currently living in Limassol, Cyprus. I focus on making data practical, clear, and useful for decision-making. I live in Cyprus with my wife and our little son.

I am a deeply curious person with many interests, and I am always looking for something new to explore. Sport has always been a constant in my life, and right now it is triathlon. Over the years, the number of my hobbies has grown so much that sometimes I do not even know where to start when people ask about them. I am a family-oriented person and happily married since 2014. In January 2026, my wife and I welcomed our son, Petr, so now I am learning to live in a new way and discovering new sides of life.

I truly love Cyprus and I am working toward Cypriot citizenship. I try to take an active part in my local community: I am a blood donor, a member of the Cyprus Triathlon Federation, and actively engaged with the island's language and culture. I recently passed Greek at B1 level.
